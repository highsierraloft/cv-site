Join systems that already had different jobs.

Cysterhood needed an in-app shopping experience within an established React Native app.

One WordPress site managed membership. A second site ran WooCommerce. A Django backend and MongoDB supported the app.

The challenge was to connect these systems while preserving the existing membership and commerce flows.

Make shopping feel like part of the app.

I delivered product browsing, product details, a cart, and checkout inside the app.

The integration connected the mobile experience to the existing WordPress and WooCommerce systems. I handled the session handoff needed to keep the journey connected.

I delivered the shopping integration in two weeks.

Keep improving after delivery.

I used Crashlytics reports to investigate problems on different devices.

I also added shopping analytics and Iterable events for product views, cart activity, and checkout starts.

This gave the team a way to inspect the shopping journey and investigate problems after release.

A complete shopping flow without replacing the systems behind it.

Roche

Enterprise deployment tooling

Learn the domain before changing the rules.

Roche needed new features within its Codebeamer environment. The work involved project configuration, workflows, and deployment rules.

I worked closely with client requirements and learned how the existing deployment process operated.

Turn requirements into enforceable behavior.

I implemented safeguards that block conflicting deployment actions before execution.

The checks considered related deployment activity and stopped an action unless the system explicitly allowed it.

I documented the decisions and their constraints so the team could review the behavior.

Make configuration differences visible.

I also built a standalone prototype that compared stored configuration revisions, including fields, permissions, and workflows.

The comparison work remained a proof of concept. It gave the team a concrete implementation to review.

Clear rules, working safeguards, and a concrete prototype for the next decision.
